### v5.2 is not a new family; it is v5.1 with one filter swapped
Its own header says "same as v5.1 but replaces EMA200 with Hull MA(55)" and "results are IDENTICAL". Trigger is still SHA flip OR Donchian(5), plus ADX >= 25, pullback gate 0.5,
wide-ATR stop (maxSL 5), RR 4, daily limit 350. Over 30 months: 133 trades (94 flip, 37 breakout, 2 both), +7,771 gross, ~breakeven after costs, 65% of profit in one month.

### v5.3 is the same family, and a good deal closer to bo(3) than to anything new
Trigger: SHA flip OR Donchian(10), EMA9/22 + EMA200 trend, ADX >= 5 (effectively off), pullback 3.0 ATR (effectively off), stops 1.0-5.0 ATR, RR 3, daily limit 350.
**918 of its 1,207 trades are breakout-triggered (plus 113 that are both)**; only 176 are pure flips.
**Independence check — correlation of monthly net P&L:** v5.3 vs bo(3) **0.95**, v5.3 vs Silver #1 **0.84**, bo(3) vs #1 0.76, v5.2 vs #1 -0.37 (a 133-trade, one-month-dominated series, so not meaningful).
Only 9-22% of entries land in the same 30-minute slot and direction as one of Silver #1's, so the timing differs, but the monthly profits move together because they ride the same silver trends.
**A genuinely different strategy would show a monthly-P&L correlation with Silver #1 near or below ~0.3. None of these does.**

### But v5.3 has a real and unusually clean result: an out-of-sample test
The 25,000-iteration sweep that produced v5.3 used only the 4.7-month Dhan cache (2026-05-06 onward). **Everything before that date is genuinely out-of-sample for it.**
| | span | trades (/mo) | PF | gross pts | after cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silver #1 | out-of-sample (to 2026-05-05) | 643 (25.3) | 1.499 | +173,636 | +134,753 |
| Silver #1 | sweep window (2026-05-06+) | 104 (22.6) | 1.520 | +48,605 | +38,610 |
| bo(3) | out-of-sample | 703 (27.7) | 1.562 | +257,883 | (flattered) |
| bo(3) | sweep window | 133 (28.9) | 1.565 | +80,058 | (flattered) |
| **v5.3** | **out-of-sample (25 months)** | **1,057 (41.6)** | **1.456** | **+182,701** | **+132,273** |
| **v5.3** | sweep window (where it was chosen) | 150 (32.6) | 1.214 | +23,836 | +9,421 |
v5.3 did **not** overfit its 4.7-month sample: its PF is better out-of-sample (1.456) than in the window it was picked on (1.214), because loose filters leave little to fit. On my
harness its sweep-window result is +23,836 (close to the header's +22,684) with 150 trades instead of 106 and PF 1.214 instead of 1.395; the header's PF does not reproduce.

### How v5.3 compares with what you have
Against Silver #1 it earns about the same points (+182,701 vs +173,636 out-of-sample; +132,273 vs +134,753 after costs) with a lower PF (1.456 vs 1.499) and about **65% more trades (41.6 vs 25.3 a month)**.
Against bo(3) it earns less (+182,701 vs +257,883 out-of-sample). It is not better than either; it is the **frequency-friendly member of the same family** (40 trades a month, above your 20/month wish).

## Bottom line
- Different family: **no.** v5.2 is v5.1 with a Hull filter (and is ~breakeven); v5.3 is a breakout-dominated version of the same stack (0.95 monthly-P&L correlation with bo(3)).
- Best silver strategy found: **bo(3)** on points (+337,941 gross; unproven, forward test running); **Silver #1** remains the incumbent; **v5.3** is the option if 40 trades a month matters more than PF.
- Crude 17:30 start: confirmed on TradingView in direction; the gain is per-trade efficiency (+6.2 -> +11.7 pts), not total points.

**On TradingView the 15:00 start is roughly a wash on points**: -6% gross (+5,327 vs +5,672), +9% after costs, PF 1.229 -> 1.260, worst-window drawdown -23%, and one more profitable window (9/13).
It does keep **20.5 trades a month**, which meets the 20-a-month wish, whereas 17:30 falls to 16. The harness had suggested more (+6,118 gross, +4,687 after cost); TradingView spans two extra months (Jan-Mar 2024) and
the replay windows differ from the harness single pass, so the harness gain for 15:00 was optimistic. Every window is small except W11 (+3,138 vs +2,608) and W12 (+31 vs +908, where 15:00 loses most).

Reading: the session tweak is about **efficiency per trade**, and it grows the later the start. 15:00 is the choice if you need frequency; 17:30 is the choice if you want the best profit per trade and the lowest drawdown.
