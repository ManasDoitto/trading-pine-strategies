# v4.0 recalibration: ADX-gate lower-threshold retry + plain-v4.0 R:R test (14 Sep 2026)

## Part 1: ADX gate at a lower threshold (18), before abandoning the idea

Per the user's request, retested the 15m ADX gate at 18 (within the brief's own "15-20 worth trying for
Silver" suggestion) on Crude and Silver -- the two cases most worth revisiting.

| Instrument | ADX threshold | Trades/mo | PF | Net pts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crude | none (baseline) | 28.3 | 1.09 | +2,136 |
| Crude | 25 | 12.3 | 1.02 | +249 |
| Crude | **18** | **20.4** | **0.99** | **-226** |
| Silver | none (baseline) | 31.9 | 1.06 | +32,815 |
| Silver | 25 | 14.8 | 1.13 | +32,982 |
| Silver | **18** | **23.7** | **1.01** | **+2,847** |

**Lowering the threshold makes things worse, not better, on both instruments.** Crude flips to net negative.
Silver's PF and net points both collapse relative to threshold=25 (net points fall by over 90%). This closes
out the "retry at a lower threshold" question with a clear answer: no, don't lower it -- 25 was actually the
better of the two settings tested, and even that didn't clear PF 1.15, let alone 1.3. **The ADX-gate idea is
now conclusively set aside for this SHA-flip signal family, at any threshold tested.**

## Part 2: Plain v4.0 (no ADX gate) recalibration -- Crude R:R 3.0 -> 4.0

Per the brief's Step 4 hypothesis ("crude trends hard enough for this"), tested R:R alone with everything
else in v4.0 unchanged.

| | Trades/mo | Win% | PF | Net pts | Windows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v4.0 baseline (RR 3.0) | 28.3 | 28.0% | 1.09 | +2,136 | 11 / 29.7mo |
| **v4.0, RR 4.0** | 26.0 | 23.5% | **1.12** | **+2,811** | 12 / 32.3mo |

**This is a genuine, real improvement** -- the first change in today's entire retuning exercise that beats
baseline v4.0 on both PF and net points, at essentially unchanged trade frequency. Win rate drops as expected
(a wider target is harder to reach), but the strategy earns enough more per winner to more than offset it.
Still short of PF 1.3, but directionally the right lever, unlike the ADX gate.
