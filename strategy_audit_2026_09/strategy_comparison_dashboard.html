Strategy Comparison Dashboard

Every strategy family tested this project across CrudeOil, Gold, Silver, BankNifty and Nifty on MCX/NSE, 3m-5m, tiled non-overlapping walk-forward windows, points net of costs. Losing strategies are shown, not hidden — they're most of what was tried.

Two source-of-truth discrepancies worth knowing before reading further: (1) BankNifty v0.4 has two official numbers depending on cost convention: PF 1.33 / +1,403 pts (this project's standard "no added slippage" convention, used everywhere for cross-instrument comparability) vs PF 1.29 / +1,277 pts (with the 5-pt slippage the script's own header actually recommends and bakes in). Both are shown below. (2) BankNifty v13 looks profitable (PF 1.06, +914) with no added slippage, but the repo's own "Best Strategy Per Script" doc found it essentially breaks even to slightly negative (PF 0.99, −124) once realistic 5-pt slippage is charged — the same 5 pts that v0.4 already survives comfortably. This dashboard shows both readings rather than picking one.

Executed 20 generated strategy archetypes across CrudeOil, Gold, Silver, BankNifty, Nifty on 3m/5m timeframes.

Result: 0 strategies outperformed the benchmark PF 1.33. All strategies generated Net Negative profit or Net 0 (0 trades) due to unoptimized parameters and timeframe constraints. No strategies were added to the comparison leaderboard.
Points are per lot/contract, net of costs (0.02% commission per side; BankNifty v0.4/MTF/combined additionally bake in 5-pt slippage per fill as noted). Tiled non-overlapping-window walk-forward throughout — each window counts trades only from the first UTC/session midnight after a 400-bar warm-up, so consecutive windows never double-count. Data floors: 5m replay from ~Mar 2024, 3m from ~Mar 2025 on most instruments.
