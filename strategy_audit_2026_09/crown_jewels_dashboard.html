Win Rate vs Profit Factor

Two axes: Win% (left, bars) and Profit Factor (right, bars) — different scales, same category.

What drives the edge — CrudeOil

Liquidity sweep + Fair Value Gap reclaim, filtered by macro trend. Finds a swing pivot (10 bars each side) or the prior day's high/low, waits for price to sweep through it (a stop-hunt wick beyond the level), then requires a displacement candle within 5 bars whose range clears 0.8× ATR(14) — the FVG. A sweep against the macro trend is discarded outright. Entry triggers on the pullback into that FVG within 10 bars. Stop sits beyond the sweep wick plus a 0.2×ATR buffer; target is either the opposing liquidity pool or a chandelier trail. Tested with the US session filter (1730–2330 IST).

What drives the edge — BankNifty

EMA pullback with a VWAP-side + volume reclaim gate, plus a higher-timeframe trend-strength filter. Price must pull back to EMA21 while EMA9 > EMA21 (or the inverse), holding VWAP-side, with a rejection wick and above-average volume on the reclaim candle, and the two fast EMAs at least 0.4×ATR(14) apart. The distinguishing gate versus every earlier variant: the previous completed 15-minute ADX(14) must be ≥ 25 — a real, already-confirmed trend on the higher timeframe, not a same-bar lookahead. Session 0930–1500 IST, forced flat 1515–1530.

Points are per lot/contract (P&L ÷ syminfo.pointvalue). Costs: 1 lot, 0.02% commission per side, no added slippage.
