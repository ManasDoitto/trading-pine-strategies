## Why the headline numbers are not reliable
1. **The Pine files take zero trades as written.** All four require `atr >= riskL`, but riskL is floored at minSL*ATR (1.5x, Silver 2.5x),
   so the gate is unsatisfiable: counted on the real bars it is true on 0 bars for crude, BankNifty and silver. The Pine `shaStable` is
   always true on a flip bar (valuewhen returns the current bar), so that filter is a no-op there, and the Silver Pine day-limit compares
   points to limit*pointvalue. Do not paste these into TradingView expecting the README numbers. (Not run on TradingView - established by
   arithmetic and the bar count.) The numbers came from `signals_v50.py`, which does not have this gate, so that is what was tested.
2. **The cited source does not exist.** All three READMEs point to `strategy_audit_2026_09/v5.0_walkforward_results.md`; that file is not in the folder.
3. **The "walk-forward" is not out-of-sample.** `walkforward_v50.py` fixes parameters chosen by a full-history sweep, then averages the PF of
   overlapping 90-day windows stepped every 30 days (each trade counted in ~3 windows). On BankNifty (original engine, current Dhan cache) that
   statistic behaves like this: mean finite window PF 8.72, median 0.80, one window with no losses (PF infinite), 11 of 22 windows positive.
   A mean of small-window PFs is dominated by a few lucky windows; the median says the typical window loses.
4. **The original engine is gross of costs** (`signals_v50.simulate` reports raw points, no commission, no gap fills). On BankNifty at
   ~55,000 a 0.02%/side round trip is ~22 pts; over 23 trades that is ~500 pts of the ~950 gross.
5. **Reproduction on the original Dhan cache** (gross, original engine, README params): the cache files were rewritten this morning
   (06:53-07:00) and now hold only 3.2 months of crude, 22.9 of BankNifty and 4.7 of silver. Crude: 9 trades, pooled PF **3.823** (README 3.82) but net
   +289.8 (README +4,677 and "32.3-mo history"). BankNifty: 23 trades, PF 1.708 (README 1.494), net +1,142.8 (README +1,807). Silver: 10 trades, PF 1.111
   (README 2.04), net +546.8 (README +3,518). Crude's PF matching to three digits on a 9-trade, 3-month sample is a strong hint about where the
   headline came from, but the net does not match, and I cannot see what the caches held when the README was written, so treat it as a hint, not a finding.

## The one number worth keeping: crude, and why it still is not a pass
Crude v5.0 (ADX 30, pullback 1.0, RR 3.0, EMA200 on): PF 1.361, +933.2 pts, DD 440.2, 155 trades, and PF > 1.1 in all three splits
(1.124 / 1.732 / 1.425), unlike the v4.0 RR 4.0 baseline measured earlier today (TRAIN 1.061 / VALID 0.955 / HOLDOUT 1.312, 33 months,
different window), and PF/DD is much better than the ancestor.
It still fails on three counts, and one caveat should weigh more than any of them:
- **The parameters were picked by a sweep on this same history**, so the three splits are not out-of-sample; this is a re-measurement of a
  tuned setting, not a validation. Nobody recorded how many combinations that sweep tried.
- Best single month = 49.8% of net; only 54.8% of months are positive; TRAIN PF 1.124 is short of 1.30.
- Net is lower than the ancestor (+933.2 vs +2,258.1) and than v4.0 RR 4.0 (+3,504.9 over 33 mo). It is a smoother, smaller strategy, not a bigger one.
Do not replace v4.0 with it. If a follow-up is wanted, the honest test is forward: shadow-book it alongside v4.0 without touching either.

## Bottom line

The README edits marking v5.0 "current best / replaces v4.0 / v0.4" are not supported by this test.
