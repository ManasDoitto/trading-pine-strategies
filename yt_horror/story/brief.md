# SHEESHA — season brief

## Logline
A man inherits his grandfather's house in a small North-Indian town, along with one rule:
**at night, do not look at yourself in the mirror. It sees you first.**

## The rule, and what it costs

The hall mirror keeps a **copy**. Every night between **2:30 and 3:00 AM** it takes one more detail
of the narrator's face — his posture, his clothes, his father's spectacles, his mother's voice. When
it holds every detail, it steps out and the original is left inside.

**Deadline: thirty nights.** One episode per night, so the season ends exactly when the copy is
complete. The audience can count with him, which is why the middle of the season escalates instead
of resetting.

## Why it is not a ghost story
The antagonist has no motivation and offers no bargain. It is not a ghost; it is a **copy that is
still being finished.** The horror is procedural — a slow, checkable countdown — and it is fully
resolvable at episode 30.

## Tone
Slow, dry, domestic. The narrator is not brave or heroic, he is tired and superstitious. Nothing
shouts. The scares are: a clock that moves backwards, a reflection a day younger, a phone call from
Gulf that knows what he just did.

## Visual grammar (faceless, no presenter, no photoreal faces)
First-person POV. The cast is **silhouettes and voices** — backlit figures, figures at the edge of
frame, and never a clear face. Recurring props: the covered mirror, the stopped clock, the mogra in
the courtyard, the well, the stairwell.

Characters are never named by face, only by **shape, prop and voice**:

| Character | Screen presence | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Narrator ("main") | camera POV | `hi-IN-Madhur` @ +6% |
| Nana (grandfather, died 6 yrs ago) | cane silhouette, recordings | `hi-IN-Madhur` @ −25%, pitch −20 Hz |
| Maa | sari pallu silhouette | `hi-IN-Swara` @ +0% |
| Papa (Gulf, phone only) | never on screen | `hi-IN-Madhur` @ +14%, pitch +8 Hz |
| The Presence | doorway silhouette, no face | `hi-IN-Swara` @ −35%, pitch −25 Hz |

A sixth character (Babulal, the old neighbour) **never speaks** — he only ever exists as a written
note, which removes the need for a sixth voice and is cheaper to shoot.

## Money and rules of the world
- The house came free with a condition attached, not bought.
- Papa left for Gulf two years before Nana died and has not returned.
- Maa has kept the mirror covered for twenty years and never explained why until episode 16.
- There is no well water, no electricity after 2 AM, and no second mirror in the house.
